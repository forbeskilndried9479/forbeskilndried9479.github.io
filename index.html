❓ What is jevry?

jevry is a free, open-source (MIT-licensed) desktop program that gives your browser the ability to act on its own. Think of it as a helpful assistant that lives inside your browser – you give it a task, and it clicks, types, searches, and navigates just like a human would. You don't need any coding or technical skills. Simply download, run, and tell jevry what you need.

Key advantages:

  • ✅ Complete website tasks – Fill forms, download reports, monitor pages, extract data – just describe what you want.
  • 📚 Cited research – Ask jevry to find sources, and it provides proper citations automatically.
  • 🎮 Play supported games – Enjoy browser-based games that jevry can launch and control.
  • 🔒 Runs locally – Everything stays on your computer – private and secure.

⚙️ Compatibility

💡 A note about Mac and Linux: While jevry is designed primarily for Windows, you may still run it through compatibility tools. But for the smoothest experience, use Windows.

🛠️ Getting Help

If you run into any issues, these quick fixes usually solve the problem:

  • I can't open the app – Make sure you've extracted all files if it came in a zip. Try right-clicking and selecting "Run as administrator."
  • Websites aren't loading – Check your internet connection. Some sites block automated tools, so try a more common site like Wikipedia or Bing.
  • The program is slow – Restart the app. Close other heavy programs. Keep your browser updated.
  • Need more help – Visit the project's GitHub page and open an issue – the community is friendly and responsive.
